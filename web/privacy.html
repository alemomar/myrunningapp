Privacy of your data

Updated October 31, 2026 · Version française

Who is responsible?

Omar Alem, creator of MyRunningApp. For any question or request: get.myrunningapp@gmail.com.

What the app keeps

  • Your account: your email address and your password, encrypted so that no one can read it.
  • Your profile: nickname, photo if you add one, age, sex, city, level, goals, availability, equipment.
  • Your runs, received from Apple Health through RunSync, or from Garmin Connect if you link your Garmin account: date, duration, distance, pace, heart rate (average, maximum and curve), resting heart rate, VO2 max, steps, power, calories.
  • What you record: how you felt after each run, out of 10. What you recorded with the previous version of the app (fatigue, mood, breathing, discomfort and pain, with the body areas concerned) remains stored.
  • Your former training plan: the sessions planned and exercises ticked off with the previous version of the app remain stored. The app no longer offers a plan.
  • The date you gave your consent.

Heart rate, how you feel and your pain are health data. The app does not keep any GPS location.

Why

To follow your progress: your summary after each run, your paces, your heart rate zone by zone and your regularity. No advertising, no resale, nothing visible to other users.

On what basis

Your explicit consent, given by ticking a box. You can withdraw it at any time. Without it, the app cannot work.

Where your data is, and who can access it

  • The database: Supabase, hosted in Ireland (European Union).
  • RunSync, on your iPhone, reads Apple Health with your permission and sends your runs to the database.
  • Garmin Connect, coming soon: if you link your Garmin account to the app, Garmin sends us your new runs. The app keeps neither the GPS track nor your location. You can remove this link at any time, in the app or from your Garmin account: Garmin then stops sending us your runs.
  • Backups: twice a day, on the controller's computer, encrypted, with an encrypted copy in the controller's Google Drive space.
  • The app's code is hosted by GitHub. It contains none of your data.
  • Who can access it: you, and the controller, helped by Claude (see below), to run and fix the app. No one else.

Artificial intelligence

The controller is helped by an artificial intelligence assistant, Claude, from Anthropic (United States), to test the app and fix errors. For this, Claude may read your runs and what you record.

  • Never to train an AI, nor for any other purpose.
  • For runs received from Garmin: only if you ticked the consent box when linking your Garmin account. You can withdraw this consent at any time, in the same place.
  • The app itself uses no AI: your summary and your progress come from fixed calculations.

How long

  • As long as your account exists.
  • If you delete your account: everything is erased from the database immediately, and from the backups within 30 days.
  • If you do not open the app for 2 years: your account is deleted.

Your rights

  • See and correct your information, in Profil (Profile).
  • Download all your data in one file, in Profil › Tes données (Profile › Your data).
  • Withdraw your consent and delete your account, in the same place.
  • For any other request, write to get.myrunningapp@gmail.com. Answer within one month.
  • You can also contact the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).

Security

Encrypted connection, password never readable, access protected by your account, encrypted backups.

Good to know

MyRunningApp does not provide medical diagnoses. If this page changes, you will be notified in the app.
